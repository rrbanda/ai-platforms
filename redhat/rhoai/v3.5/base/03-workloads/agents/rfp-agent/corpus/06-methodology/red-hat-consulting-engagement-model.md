# Red Hat Consulting — Engagement Model & Delivery Methodology

## Overview

Red Hat Consulting provides hands-on collaboration, mentorship, and technical expertise to help organizations adopt Red Hat technology and open source practices. Engagements are designed to create customer independence — not long-term dependency on external services.

## Engagement Types

### 1. Advisory & Architecture Services

- **Architecture Review & Assessment**: Infrastructure assessment producing gap analysis, risk register, and remediation roadmap (2-4 days, Architect/Principal level)
- **Dedicated Architecture Service**: Embedded architect providing ongoing guidance, roadmap development, and design validation
- **Strategic Roadmap Development**: Technology investment planning aligned to business objectives

### 2. Implementation & Delivery

- **Platform Deployment**: Greenfield or brownfield deployments (OpenShift, RHEL, Ansible, Satellite)
- **Security Hardening**: SCAP-based hardening against CIS Benchmarks, DISA STIG, organizational standards (3-8 days)
- **Automation Strategy**: Building automation frameworks with Ansible Automation Platform
- **Migration**: CentOS/Oracle Linux → RHEL, VM → container, legacy → cloud-native

### 3. Open Innovation Labs (Residency)

Immersive 4-12 week engagements:
- One-on-one pairing of Red Hat experts with customer engineers
- No prescribed methodologies — adapted to specific challenges
- Pair programming, retrospectives, showcases
- Demo Day: Team presents process, work, and results to stakeholders
- Goal: Customer independence and self-sufficient teams

### 4. Dedicated Services

- **Dedicated Enterprise Engineer**: Long-term embedded technical resource
- **Dedicated Architect**: Ongoing architecture guidance and planning support
- **Security TAM**: Dedicated security technical account manager

## Delivery Methodology

### Standard Engagement Lifecycle

```
Discovery → Design → Deliver → Transfer
```

**Discovery (1-2 weeks)**:
- Business requirements alignment
- Technical architecture assessment
- Level of effort estimation
- Resource planning and role identification
- Scoping call and Statement of Work

**Design (2-4 weeks)**:
- Solution architecture and roadmap
- Proof of concept / prototype
- Risk identification and mitigation planning
- Success criteria definition

**Deliver (variable)**:
- Hands-on implementation alongside customer teams
- Iterative delivery with sprint cadences
- Regular showcases and stakeholder updates
- Documentation and evidence capture

**Transfer (1-2 weeks)**:
- Knowledge transfer sessions
- Runbook and documentation handover
- Team enablement and certification paths
- Sustainability planning

### Governance Cadence

- **Daily**: Stand-ups with delivery team
- **Weekly**: Status reporting, risk review, decision log updates
- **Bi-weekly**: Stakeholder showcase / sprint review
- **Monthly**: Executive steering committee, KPI review

## Differentiation

### Why Red Hat Consulting vs. Generic SI

1. **Product expertise**: Consultants build and contribute to the products they implement
2. **Upstream access**: Direct connection to Red Hat engineering for escalation
3. **Open source methodology**: Practices honed from decades of open source community experience
4. **Customer independence**: Explicit goal of enabling self-sufficiency, not creating dependency
5. **Global knowledge base**: Blueprints tested across thousands of customer engagements worldwide
6. **Training integration**: Seamless path to Red Hat Training and Certification for team upskilling

### Engagement Flexibility

- Consultants collaborate with customer teams (not isolated delivery)
- No prescribed methodologies — adapted to each organization's needs
- Mix of on-site and remote delivery
- Scale up/down based on workload phases
