## Open Source Model

Red Hat follows an upstream-first development model. All product code originates in community-driven open source projects (e.g., Fedora → RHEL, OKD → OpenShift, Ansible community → Ansible Automation Platform). Red Hat engineers contribute directly to upstream projects, and product releases are built from tested, hardened snapshots of upstream code.

## IBM Relationship

Red Hat operates as an independent subsidiary of IBM. Red Hat maintains its own:

- Product roadmap and release cadence
- Engineering organization and hiring
- Brand identity and go-to-market
- Open source community relationships

Enterprise customers can bundle Red Hat subscriptions with IBM enterprise agreements.
