# Red Hat Support Tiers and SLAs

## Support Tiers Overview

| Tier | Availability | Channels | Cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-Support | N/A | Knowledgebase, portal tools | No engineer-assisted cases |
| Standard | Business hours (local time zone) | Web, phone | Unlimited |
| Premium | 24x7 for Sev1/Sev2; business hours for Sev3/Sev4 | Web, phone | Unlimited |

## Severity Definitions

| Severity | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Sev1 | Urgent | Production system down or severely impacted, no workaround available |
| Sev2 | High | Production functionality severely reduced, no workaround available |
| Sev3 | Medium | Partial, non-critical loss of functionality |
| Sev4 | Low | General usage question, documentation clarification, minor issue |

## Initial Response Time SLAs

### Standard Tier

| Severity | Initial Response Time |
|---|---|
| Sev1 | 1 business hour |
| Sev2 | 4 business hours |
| Sev3 | 1 business day |
| Sev4 | 2 business days |

### Premium Tier

| Severity | Initial Response Time | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Sev1 | 1 hour | 24x7 |
| Sev2 | 2 hours | 24x7 (must be explicitly requested) |
| Sev3 | 4 business hours | Business hours |
| Sev4 | 8 business hours | Business hours |

**Note:** Premium Sev2 24x7 coverage must be explicitly requested when filing the case. Without explicit request, Sev2 cases follow business-hours handling.

## Self-Support Tier Details

Self-Support subscriptions include:

- Access to the Red Hat Customer Portal knowledgebase
- Errata and software updates via portal
- Red Hat Insights (basic)
- No access to support engineers for case resolution

## Additional Support Options

### Enhanced Solution Support

Available for Red Hat OpenShift and Red Hat OpenStack Platform. Provides dedicated support engineers with deep product specialization and prioritized case handling.

### Technical Account Manager (TAM)

Optional add-on to any Premium subscription. Provides:

- Named technical resource assigned to the account
- Proactive guidance on architecture, upgrades, and best practices
- Quarterly business reviews
- Escalation advocacy

### Management Escalation

Two methods for escalating active cases:

1. **Case escalation button** — available in the Customer Portal on the case detail page
2. **Phone escalation** — contact the regional support center and reference the case number
