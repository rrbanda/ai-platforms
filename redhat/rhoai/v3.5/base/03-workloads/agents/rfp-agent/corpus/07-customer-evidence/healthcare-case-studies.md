## Cigna Healthcare

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Annual claims processed | 1.5 billion |
| Infrastructure | Red Hat Enterprise Linux, Red Hat OpenShift |

- One of the largest US health insurance companies by claims volume.
- Red Hat infrastructure underpins claims processing, member services, and provider network management.
- **HIPAA compliance** achieved via SELinux mandatory access controls, comprehensive audit logging, and Red Hat's FIPS 140-2 validated cryptographic modules.
- Automated compliance scanning with OpenSCAP.

## Health Info Net — Switzerland

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Platform | Red Hat OpenShift (managed by VSHN) |
| Use case | Sovereign container platform for patient records |
| Year | 2026 |

- Secure container platform for Swiss patient health records.
- **Data sovereignty:** All patient data remains within Swiss jurisdiction, compliant with Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).
- Deployed and managed by VSHN, a Swiss-based managed OpenShift provider.
- Encryption at rest and in transit. Role-based access control integrated with Swiss healthcare identity federation.
