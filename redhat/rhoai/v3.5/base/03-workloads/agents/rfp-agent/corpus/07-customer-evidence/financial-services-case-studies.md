# Financial Services Customer Evidence — Red Hat Security & Automation

## Overview

Red Hat has a proven track record delivering security, compliance, and automation solutions to the world's largest financial institutions. These case studies demonstrate measurable outcomes in environments with stringent regulatory requirements similar to those in enterprise vulnerability remediation engagements.

## Case Study 1: Banco Bradesco — Multicluster Policy Governance

**Customer**: Banco Bradesco, one of Brazil's largest financial groups
**Challenge**: 90+ OpenShift clusters across on-premises, Azure, and IBM Cloud with decentralized management, inconsistent standards, and configuration drift creating operational risk
**Solution**: Red Hat OpenShift Platform Plus with Advanced Cluster Management

### Results

- **130 clusters** under centralized policy management
- **80+ custom policies** developed as code to automate governance
- **100% settings managed by code** — eliminated configuration drift
- **88% reduction** in change cycle time
- **Self-remediation**: Automated correction of policy violations
- **NIST compliance** enforced via Compliance Operator across all clusters
- **ServiceNow integration**: Auto-generates tickets for policy violations

### Key Methodology

- Installed Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management January 2024
- Policy development began one month later
- Centralized metrics: Prometheus → Azure Blob Storage → Grafana
- Policy-as-code approach ensures consistent governance at scale

**Relevance**: Demonstrates Red Hat's ability to manage security policy at enterprise scale with automated enforcement and audit-ready evidence.
